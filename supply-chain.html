Project Description

The Supply Chain Management System is a web-based application developed to improve the efficiency of inventory tracking, logistics operations, and product movement across the supply chain. The system provides a centralized platform for monitoring stock levels, managing suppliers, tracking shipments, and maintaining accurate inventory records.

By integrating inventory management and logistics tracking into a single solution, the platform helps businesses reduce operational delays, prevent stock shortages, and optimize resource utilization. Users can monitor product availability in real time, track incoming and outgoing shipments, and generate valuable insights for better decision-making.

This project demonstrates the practical application of web development, database management, and process automation techniques to solve real-world supply chain challenges. It highlights how digital transformation can improve productivity, transparency, and overall operational performance within modern business environments.
