Project Description

The Dynamic Pricing Engine is an intelligent pricing system designed to automatically adjust product prices based on market conditions, customer demand, competitor pricing, and inventory levels. The system uses data-driven algorithms to determine optimal pricing strategies that maximize revenue while maintaining competitiveness.

By continuously analyzing real-time and historical market data, the engine identifies pricing opportunities and responds to changing business environments. The system helps organizations improve profit margins, increase sales performance, and make informed pricing decisions without constant manual intervention.

This project demonstrates the application of data analytics, machine learning concepts, and business intelligence techniques in solving real-world pricing challenges. It showcases how automation and predictive analysis can support smarter commercial decision-making in modern digital marketplaces.
